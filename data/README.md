
# Project Datasets

This directory contains the datasets used for the Enterprise Sales & Operations Analytics project.

## Planned Datasets

- sales_data.csv - Daily sales transactions
- product_data.csv - Product information and pricing
- inventory_data.csv - Inventory levels and movements
- procurement_data.csv - Purchase orders and supplier information
- labor_data.csv - Staffing hours and labor costs

## Data Source

The project will use synthetic business data designed to simulate a multi-location retail and food-service operation.

No confidential employer data will be used.

## Business Applications

These datasets will support:

- Sales and revenue analysis
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) analysis
- Inventory optimization
- Procurement performance monitoring
- Labor cost analysis
- Executive KPI dashboards
